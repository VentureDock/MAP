FAQ

Common questions before you apply.

What timezone are sessions held in?

Sessions are typically scheduled in Pacific Time, chosen to support participation across multiple regions. Final timings are shared before the cohort begins.

Do I need to be incorporated in the US already?

No. A dedicated session helps you understand entity options, legal readiness and exactly when it makes sense to set up a US presence.

How much time per week does this require?

Around 2 to 3 hours per week, including live sessions, homework, and applying the frameworks to your business.

What happens if I miss a session?

Sessions are recorded where possible and materials are shared, but attending live gets you the full value of mentor feedback and peer discussion.

Is Demo Day only for investors?

Demo Day introduces you to a curated audience of investors, angels, operators and ecosystem partners we invite for the cohort.

Are the perk credits really included?

Yes. Perk-partner credits unlock when you join, so you can start deploying value from home before you land. The exact stack is finalized per cohort and reaches up to $200,000 in total value.

When is the application deadline?

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until the cohort fills. With seats capped at 20, early applications are strongly encouraged.

Will Venture Dock make introductions in the US?

You get access to relevant mentors, operators, investors and ecosystem partners. Introductions are facilitated where there's a clear fit, though outcomes aren't guaranteed.
